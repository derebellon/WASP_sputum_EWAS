# paper_final/

Curated selection of the figures and tables that go into the manuscript. This
folder is filled in by hand: copy the chosen outputs here from results/ and
figures/ once they are final.

- main/            Main-text figures and tables.
- supplementary/   Supplementary figures and tables (all inflammatory-phenotype
                   analyses belong here).

The rest of results/ and figures/ is regenerated by the pipeline; this folder is
the stable, hand-picked set for the paper.
